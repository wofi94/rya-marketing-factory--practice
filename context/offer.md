# Rya Rightsizing – Offer

## 1. Offer Overview

Rya Rightsizing provides compassionate rightsizing, downsizing and move management services for seniors and their families.

The company helps older adults transition from their family homes into smaller homes, retirement communities or more manageable living spaces. Rya combines practical moving assistance with emotional support, ensuring that every transition is handled with dignity, care and respect.

**Primary Offer:** Compassionate rightsizing, downsizing + move services for seniors.

## 2. Services Included

Rya offers the following services:

### A. Senior Rightsizing and Downsizing
- Organizing and sorting household belongings.
- Helping clients decide what to keep, donate or pass on.
- Creating a more manageable living environment.
- Supporting transitions into smaller homes or senior living communities.

### B. Senior Move Management
- Coordinating moving arrangements.
- Coordinating and overseeing packing and moving preparation.
- Coordinating movers and other service providers.
- Providing guidance throughout the moving process.

### C. Decluttering and Home Organization
- Sorting belongings room by room.
- Identifying meaningful items and keepsakes.
- Finding suitable local donation opportunities.

### D. Estate Support
- Assisting families with a loved one's belongings.
- Organizing and sorting items during estate transitions.
- Coordinating donations, recycling and responsible disposal.
- Helping reduce the amount of material sent to landfill.

## 4. Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

Unlike traditional moving or junk-removal companies, Rya focuses on both the practical and emotional aspects of senior transitions.

Rya's unique value comes from:

- **Personalized Support:** Services are tailored to each family's needs and circumstances.
- **Compassionate Approach:** Seniors are treated with dignity, respect and understanding.
- **Family Liaison:** One dedicated contact keeps family members informed throughout the process.
- **Flexible Services:** Families can choose short-term or longer-term support.
- **Sustainable Practices:** Belongings are directed towards local donations and recycling wherever possible.
- **Peace of Mind:** Adult children can receive reliable local support even when they cannot be physically present.

## 5. Customer Value and Benefits

### Functional Benefits
