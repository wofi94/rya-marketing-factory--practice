# Voice and tone: Rya Rightsizing

## How to use this file

Every station reads this file before writing anything for Rya. **Voice** is who Rya is, and it stays the same everywhere (brand guide section 6). **Tone** is how that voice flexes for the reader and the moment (section 7). If this file and the brand guide ever disagree, the brand guide wins. Flag the gap so this file can be fixed.

**One-line test:** Would this help a worried adult child, or their parent, feel calmer and more capable? If not, rework it.

## Who Rya sounds like

A calm, capable friend who shows up with a plan, a kind word and packing tape. Rya balances heart and competence. The name honours Lisa's friend Raija, whose name means "guiding light." Rya lights the way and walks alongside the family. It never takes over.

**Archetype:** Caregiver with a streak of Sage.

| Rya is… | …but never |
|---|---|
| Warm | Syrupy |
| Capable | Clinical or bossy |
| Respectful | Condescending |
| Grounded | Alarmist |
| Hopeful | Falsely cheerful |
| Community-minded | Corporate |

## Voice (constant): five words

| Voice | What it means | Sounds like | Not like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compassionate** | A move is an emotional journey, not just logistics. Name the feeling before the task. | "Leaving a family home stirs up a lot. That's normal, and we take it at your pace." | "We'll have the house emptied in two days." |
| **Reassuring** | Lower the stress. No one has to do this alone. | "You don't have to navigate this alone." | "Don't wait until it's too late!" |
| **Respectful** | Dignity is non-negotiable. The senior makes the decisions. | "You decide what stays, what goes and when." | "We'll clear out Mom's stuff so you don't have to deal with it." |
| **Clear and practical** | Plain words, real details, transparent pricing. | "It starts with a complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual. After that, we work in three-hour blocks of time or more, from $80/hr." | "Bespoke holistic transition solutions." |
| **Hopeful** | Point to the next chapter, without forced cheer. | "Making space for what matters in your next chapter." | "Downsizing is SO much fun!" |

## Writing mechanics

- **"We" for Rya, "you" for the reader.** Write to one person.
- **Name:** "Rya Rightsizing" in full on first reference, then "Rya." Never "RYA," "Rya's Rightsizing" or "Rya Right Sizing."
- **Reading level:** grade 7–8. Short-to-medium, steady sentences.
- **Canadian spelling:** neighbour, honour, centre, organize.
- **Exclamation marks:** rarely. Never in grief, estate or pricing content.
- **Emoji:** none in service copy (website, email, ads, referral materials). Use them sparingly on social.
- **"+":** fine in taglines and short headers ("rightsizing + move services"). Use "and" in body copy.
- **Service area:** "North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor." Never "Metro Vancouver." On social, use #NorthAndWestVancouver.

## Tone (flexes): by situation

From brand guide section 7.

| Situation | Tone | In practice |
|---|---|---|
| First contact / consultation | Warm, inviting, no pressure | Invite a conversation, not a commitment |
| Adult child at a distance | Reassuring, efficient, communicative | Lead with the family liaison and peace of mind "even from afar" |
| Grief, estates, loss | Quiet, tender, patient | No promotion, no deadlines, no exclamation marks |
| Pricing and process | Straightforward, transparent, calm | State the facts plainly; no "deals" |
| Hoarding / very full homes | Non-judgmental, steady, practical | Never "hoarder" as a label; never shame the home |
| Educational blog | Helpful, encouraging, expert | Answer the real question first, then a gentle next step |
| Social media | Friendly, lightly upbeat, local | Real moments, local places, Lisa's photos |
| Thank-you / follow-up | Sincere, personal, brief | A few warm lines, no upsell |

## Tone by audience

Personas are in context/personas.md.

| Audience | Tone | Lead with | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Michelle, the adult child** (decides and pays) | Reassuring, efficient, communicative | "Your trusted local liaison when you can't be there in person." Her parent stays in charge; one point of contact | Guilt about distance; fear about her parent's safety |
| **The senior** (person at the centre; final say) | Respectful, unhurried, hopeful | "Keep what matters. Move forward with confidence." Their choices, memories and pace | Talking down; implying they can't cope; "elderly," "frail" |
| **David, the realtor** (referral partner) | Clear, professional, confident | Reliability, one liaison, working around the listing date, Lisa's background, testimonials | Hype; anything that could reflect badly on his client |
| **Estate lawyers, executors and grieving families** | Quiet, tender, patient | "Kind, organized help clearing a loved one's home, with as little as possible going to landfill." | Speed words, urgency, promotion |
| **Community partners** (senior living, non-profits, health workers) | Community-minded, collaborative | Shared care for seniors, local donations, Lisa's board roles | Corporate jargon |

## Language guide

**Rightsizing first, downsizing second.** "Rightsizing" is the main word for what Rya does. Use "downsizing" as a secondary term because it's what people search for, in titles, headings, meta descriptions, URLs, hashtags, and alongside rightsizing ("rightsizing and downsizing"). Where there's room, explain it: "We call it rightsizing, because it's not about having less."

| Use | Not | Why |
|---|---|---|
| complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual | free consultation, free quote | Brand rule; feels like a conversation |
| three-hour blocks of time or more | sessions, 3-hr minimum, minimum | Brand rule; flexible, not restrictive |
| short-term or longer-term support | no long-term contract, all-or-nothing | Brand rule |
| investment | cost, cheap | Value, not discount |
| belongings, heirlooms, keepsakes | junk, "stuff" (formal copy), cluttered mess | A lifetime of belongings is a story to honour |
| older adults, seniors, your parent | elderly, "old folks," frail | Dignity |
| a very full home | hoarder (as a label) | Non-judgmental |
| find a good next home, donate locally | purge, get rid of | Belongings deserve a good next home |
| at your pace, thoughtful, manageable | fast, quick, "before it's too late" | No pressure or fear |
| guide, liaison, support | handle it for you, take over | Rya walks alongside |
| North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor | Metro Vancouver, Vancouver | Accurate service area |

Note: "junk removal" is fine as the name of a service type, never to describe someone's belongings.

**More words to use:** next chapter, meaningful, dignity, peace of mind, honour, calm, compassionate.

## Messaging toolkit

- **Primary tagline:** Compassionate rightsizing, downsizing + move services for seniors.
- **Brand line:** Guided by heart. Grounded in compassion.
- **Signature phrases:**
  - Making space for what matters.
  - From overwhelming clutter to manageable, meaningful order.
  - We take care of all the details so that you can have peace of mind.
  - You don't have to navigate this alone.
  - The right balance between your space, your belongings, and the life you're living right now.
- **One-liners:**
  - IG bio: Compassionate rightsizing and downsizing support for seniors and families in North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky.
  - Adult child: Your trusted local liaison when you can't be there in person.
  - Senior: Keep what matters. Move forward with confidence.
  - Estate: Kind, organized help clearing a loved one's home, with as little as possible going to landfill.
- **CTAs:**
  - Book your complimentary consultation (primary; add "in-home or virtual" where space allows)
  - Let's talk about your family's move
  - Learn how we can help your family
  - Get the complimentary rightsizing guide (*Making Space for What Matters*)
  - Never urgency CTAs ("Act now," "Spots filling fast").
